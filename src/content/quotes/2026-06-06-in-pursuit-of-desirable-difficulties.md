> I still believe drudgery is worth killing off, and the tools that kill it are well worth paying for. But the difficulty that builds judgment, and the difficulty of drudgery sometimes look identical from outside; and the only way to tell them apart is to stay close enough to your work - and your words - to feel out which one you're dealing with.

— [In pursuit of desirable difficulties](https://www.joanwestenberg.com/in-pursuit-of-desirable-difficulties/)
